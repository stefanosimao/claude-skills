# Sources — bonsai-care

Trusted sources for the verification and new-species research steps in SKILL.md (steps 5 and "Adding a new species"). Prefer these over generic gardening sites and SEO content farms; bonsai-specific timing and technique often contradict general houseplant advice.

## Trusted for web verification 🔗
- **Bonsai Empire** (bonsaiempire.com) — broadest structured species guides and technique articles; good first check.
- **Bonsai4me** — Harry Harrington (bonsai4me.com) — practitioner-written species guides and progression articles; strong on temperate species and repotting/pruning timing.
- **Bonsai Mirai library** (bonsaimirai.com) — Ryan Neil; deepest on horticultural mechanism (water/oxygen balance, vascular physiology); use when the "why" needs grounding.
- **American Bonsai Society** (absbonsai.org) and national/regional club resources — conservative, climate-aware baselines.
- **University extension services** (e.g. *.edu extension pages) — for pathogen/pest identification (Phytophthora, spider mites, scale) rather than bonsai technique.

## Cross-check rule
For contested calls (repotting frequency, root-pruning aggressiveness, misting), require agreement of ≥2 independent sources above before stating as fact; otherwise present the range per the Voice & stance section.
